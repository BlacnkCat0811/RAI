Same device, backend, build and method as `20261106-real-device-s60-first-import.txt`:
each sample resets only the local disposable dataset and the iPhone performs a genuine first
capture and first import of the same real, read-only Workout. n=20, none discarded.

## Why this is not a P02 (S120) sample

The only record at or above 100 minutes in this library stores its measurements as HealthKit
quantity *series* rather than individual samples: 15,335 series points and 6,480 route points,
but only a handful of archived quantity samples. Its canonical bundle is 1,669,722 bytes, i.e.
**about one fifth** of the plan's S120 profile (8,270,561 bytes of synthetic JSON) and even
smaller than the S60-class real record (8,565,743 bytes). It therefore exercises a different
cost shape, and P02 stays NOT VERIFIED: the library has no real record with S120-class density.
