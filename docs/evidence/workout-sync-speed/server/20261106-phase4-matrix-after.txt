## Reading

- `strict_decode_validation_ms` dominates at every tier (1799.6 ms at S60, 7153.6 ms at S240) and matches the `json_decode_ms` share of the dedicated S60 acknowledgement breakdown. Strict decoding is the single largest server-side cost.
- `validation_ms` (semantic validation, now executed once inside the Store) is 100.7 ms at S60 and 400.4 ms at S240 — the deduplication removed a second full traversal, not the remaining one.
- `http_ack_ms` includes client-side bundle assembly, transport and import, so it is NOT `T_server_ack`; use `20261106-sync-speed-server-ack-s60.txt` for the acknowledgement-only measurement.
- The 240 min tier now completes: the plain upload returned 200 and the gzip identity replay returned the original receipt. Before the statement-timeout fix this tier failed with 503.
